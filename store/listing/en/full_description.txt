Forget to drink water? Not anymore.

Daily Water Reminder works out how much water you need every day and gently reminds you to drink it, from the moment you wake up until bedtime. Meet Bob, a cheerful water drop who cheers you on and reacts to your progress.

HOW MUCH SHOULD I DRINK?
• Calculate your daily goal from your weight and activity level
• Or set your own goal manually (900 to 6120 ml)
• See at a glance how much is left for today

REMINDERS THAT FIT YOUR DAY
• Choose when your day starts and ends, and reminders stay inside that window
• Automatic interval based on your goal, or pick every 30, 60, 90 or 120 minutes
• Reminders stop for the day once you reach your goal
• Skips a reminder if you just had a drink
• Log your drink or snooze for 15 minutes straight from the notification, no need to open the app
• Reminders keep working after a restart or an app update

LOG WATER IN ONE TAP
• One-tap add with your favourite glass size (150, 200, 250, 330, 500 ml or any size you type)
• Quick amounts and a custom amount
• Undo a mistake instantly, swipe to delete entries
• Today's log with the time of every drink

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Last 7 days chart with your goal line
• Current streak and best streak
• Daily average and how many days you hit your goal

BOB, YOUR HYDRATION BUDDY
• A friendly water drop with moods: happy, sleepy, surprised, in love and more
• Funny reminder messages that make drinking water a little less boring
• Tap Bob for a new tip

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Smooth animated water that rises as you drink
• Light and dark themes
• Available in English and Turkish
• No account and no sign-up. Your drink log stays on your device.

The app is free and supported by a small banner ad.

Please note: the daily amount is a general estimate and not medical advice. Your needs may vary with health, climate and activity. If you have a medical condition, talk to your doctor.

Stay hydrated. Bob believes in you!
